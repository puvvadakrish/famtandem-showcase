# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-18*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** FamTandem turns family logistics into shared coverage.

**What it does:** FamTandem captures family commitments from emails, PDFs, calendar invites, registrations, and manual entry, turns them into structured events, syncs them to existing calendars, and tracks who owns each event. It focuses on the logistics layer calendars miss: who saw the update, who is dropping off, who is picking up, what changed, and what is still uncovered.

**Product category:** Family logistics, family coordination, shared family calendar add-on, household operating system.

**Product type:** Consumer subscription app / family SaaS.

**Business model:** Likely $8-15/month per household after validation; exact pricing TBD.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Not B2B; target households.

**Decision-makers:** The overloaded default parent in a high-complexity household.

**Primary use case:** Managing kids' school, sports, enrichment, appointments, forms, reminders, and last-minute changes without one parent carrying the full coordination load.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Turn scattered school/activity inputs into reliable family events.
- Make every event's ownership and coverage status visible.
- Reduce repeated spouse/caregiver negotiation about who is handling what.

**Use cases:**
- Forward a school email or PDF and extract events.
- Detect a soccer practice time/location change and ask for reconfirmation.
- Assign drop-off and pickup separately.
- Review a weekly coverage view for uncovered items and conflicts.

## Personas

| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Default parent | Not being the only person who remembers everything | School/activity details live across inboxes, texts, apps, PDFs, and memory | Stop carrying family logistics alone |

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Families do not have a calendar problem; they have a logistics and coverage problem.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Shared calendars show what is happening, but not who owns the work around it.
- Family organizers often add lists, chores, and chat without solving coverage.
- Text threads and screenshots create awareness but lose source-of-truth status.

**What it costs them:** Time, stress, missed updates, repeated negotiation, last-minute scrambles, preventable conflicts, and uneven mental load.

**Emotional tension:** "I am the only one who knows what needs to happen."

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Cozi, Skylight Calendar, Jam, Howbout, Famnly — useful shared family calendars and organizers, but they cluster around visibility rather than coverage ownership.

**Secondary:** Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook — entrenched calendar systems that show events but do not manage family handoffs, changes, or unresolved coverage.

**Indirect:** Text threads, forwarded emails, screenshots, paper flyers, memory, weekly partner conversations — flexible but fragile.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Family inbox for logistics inputs.
- AI extraction with source and confidence.
- Coverage state for every event.
- Split ownership for drop-off and pickup.
- Change detection that requires reconfirmation.
- Weekly coverage view, not just a calendar view.

**How we do it differently:** FamTandem sits above existing calendars instead of replacing them. It turns incoming commitments into source-backed events with ownership and unresolved states.

**Why that's better:** Families keep their calendar habits while getting the missing layer: who owns what, what changed, and what still needs a decision.

**Why customers choose us:** They want relief from invisible coordination work, not another place to manually maintain a schedule.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| We already use Google/Apple Calendar | FamTandem syncs to your calendar; it manages the coverage layer calendars miss. |
| I do not want another app to maintain | The wedge is forwarding and extraction, so parents type less, not more. |
| I do not trust AI with family details | Every extracted event keeps the source and confidence level for review. |

**Anti-persona:** Low-complexity households with one child, few activities, no caregiver coordination, and little pain around family logistics.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Too many school/activity inputs, repeated "did you see this?" conversations, missed changes, and one parent carrying the calendar mentally.

**Pull:** A shared logistics layer that makes coverage visible and prompts action.

**Habit:** Existing calendars, text threads, and one parent manually forwarding/copying everything.

**Anxiety:** Another app to maintain, privacy of family data, AI mistakes, and whether the other parent will actually use it.

**Words to use:** coverage, covered, uncovered, logistics, handoff, source, confidence, family inbox, weekly coverage, default parent, mental load, who owns it.

**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
